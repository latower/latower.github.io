## Current

### Algorithms for NP-hard Problems

I am currently the responsible professor for TU Delft's 3rd-year Bachelor course on [Algorithms for NP-hard Problems (CSE3300)](https://studiegids.tudelft.nl/courses/study-guide/educations/33538), which we offer in Q3 as part of the BSc Computer Science and Engineering programme. My co-lecturers are [Dr. Emir Demirović](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/software-technology/algorithmics/people/emir-demirovic) and [Dr. Anna Lukina](https://annalukina.com/).

### Probabilistic Models & Inference

[Dr. Julia Olkhovskaia](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/i.m.olkhovskaia/) and I are co-responsible professors for TU Delft's 1st-year Master course on [Probabilistic Models and Inference (DSAIT4105)](https://studiegids.tudelft.nl/courses/study-guide/educations/34192), which we offer in Q2 as part of the MSc Data Science and Artificial Intelligence Technology programme. My other co-lecturers are [Dr. Sebastijan Dumančić](https://sebdumancic.github.io/) and [Prof. Dr. Frans Oliehoek](https://sebdumancic.github.io/).
