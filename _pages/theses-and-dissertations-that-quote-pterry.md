`GNU Terry Pratchett`

This is an incomplete list of MSc theses, PhD theses and doctoral dissertations that quote Sir Terry Pratchett. If you have any recommendations, please contact me.

*In ascending order of year of publication.*

[Dingjan, J.](https://mendeddrum.org/@happydisciple), [Multi-mode optical resonators and wave chaos](https://qowiki.physics.leidenuniv.nl/_media/website/dingjan_thesis.pdf), June 2003, Leiden University. The cover of this thesis features the quantum weather butterfly that is first mentioned in *Interesting Times*. See [this post](https://mendeddrum.org/@happydisciple/116486672525751931) for more info. 

Latour, A.L.D., [Optimal decision-making under constraints and uncertainty](https://scholarlypublications.universiteitleiden.nl/handle/1887/3455662), Sept 2022, Leiden University. Motto for the Introduction, on page 1, is from *Feet of Clay*.
