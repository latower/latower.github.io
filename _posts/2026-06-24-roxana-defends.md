Great news! Yesterday [ir. Roxana Băcălie](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ioana-roxana-bacalie/) successfully defended her MSc thesis: [Evaluating the Communication Reliability of Earth-Moon Satellite Constellations Using Model Counting](https://resolver.tudelft.nl/uuid:7b6840fc-3ebc-4c1f-ad33-e4e304859c62)!

![Roxana signs her diploma. She is smiling as she does it, and a red certificate tube that will hold the diploma is on the table next to her. The table is covered in a cyan blue TU Delft table cloth, and behind her is a cyan blue TU Delft banner.](/images/2026/2026-06-23_roxana_crop_25percent.JPG){: .align-center}

The idea for this project was born during a Dagstuhl seminar on [Optimization and Automated Reasoning for Designing Future Space Missions](https://www.dagstuhl.de/de/seminars/seminar-calendar/seminar-details/25362), where I met [Dr. Max Bannach](https://www.esa.int/gsp/ACT/team/max_bannach/) from the [European Space Agency](https://www.esa.int/)'s (ESA) [Advanced Concepts Team](https://www.esa.int/gsp/ACT/) (ACT). Motivated by the recent interest in Lunar missions (*e.g.*, [ispace's HAKUTO-R missions](https://ispace-inc.com/m2) and [NASA's Artemis missions](https://www.nasa.gov/humans-in-space/artemis/)), and the pollution of the night sky with many satellites (at least on my side), we wanted to design a robust satellite communication network for the Moon-Earth system, using a minimal number of deployed satellites. A challenging problem that requires combinatorial reasoning over uncertainty in a dynamically changing system.

While Roxana did not manage to solve the entire problem, she delivered a solid contribution that future MSc students (or other researchers) will build on. She investigated the influence that different ways of modelling such a Moon-Earth system of communication satellites have on her ability to reason over the system. Here, she used inter-satellite distance as the key component to computing the probability that two satellites can communicate with each other.

As always, it was also a pleasure to work with Roxana's other supervisor, [Dr. Neil Yorke-Smith](https://www.tudelft.nl/ewi/over-de-faculteit/afdelingen/software-technology/algorithmics/people/neil-yorke-smith), her mentor [Dr. Max Bannach](https://www.esa.int/gsp/ACT/team/max_bannach/), and to learn from the input of her external examiner, [Prof. Dr. Frans Oliehoek](https://www.tudelft.nl/staff/f.a.oliehoek/).

However: the most important collaboration was of course with Roxana. I much enjoyed working with her. She was keen to deliver a high-quality model, and did not stop asking questions until she understood the important details. I appreciated her honesty and her contributions to the research group. I am sure that she will do great in her new role as Software Engineer at TomTom!
